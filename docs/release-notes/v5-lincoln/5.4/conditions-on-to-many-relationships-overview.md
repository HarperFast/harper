---
section: 'Conditions on To-Many Relationships'
order: 10
---

[Make each relationship condition existential, and add relationship[...] for conditions one related record must meet](https://github.com/HarperFast/harper/pull/3927) makes each AND condition on a to-many relationship be met by some related record, possibly a different one for each condition, as on an array attribute, regardless of which condition Harper evaluates first: `GET /Brand/?products.name=Keyboard&products.name=Cable` returns a brand that has both. To require one related record to meet several conditions, put them in brackets after the relationship name: `GET /Brand/?products[name=Keyboard&price=ge=30]` returns a brand with a Keyboard that costs at least 30. Programmatically, the group is `{ attribute: 'products', conditions: [...] }`. Groups can use `|`, nest another relationship (`departments[employees[tag=oncall&level=ge=3]]`), and follow a path (`departments.employees[tag=oncall&level=ge=3]`). `select()` of the relationship returns the related records that meet any one of the AND conditions, a group counting as one condition, and a relationship condition inside an OR decides which parents match without narrowing the selection.

**Upgrade impact:** before 5.4, whether separate conditions on one to-many relationship had to be met by one related record depended on which condition Harper evaluated first. A query or collection `DELETE` with such conditions can now match more parents: use the bracket form where one related record must meet every condition, and check a destructive filter with a `GET` first. `select()` of the relationship can return more related records. A request with a relationship condition inside an OR can return more parents and more related records. Brackets after a relationship name among the conditions, such as `products[name=Cable]`, used to apply their conditions to the queried table; they now apply to the related records, and brackets after a name that is not a relationship return HTTP 400. See [Conditions on a To-Many Relationship](/reference/v5/rest/querying#conditions-on-a-to-many-relationship) and [relationship groups](/reference/v5/resources/resource-api#conditions-on-a-to-many-relationship).

This change is not released yet.
